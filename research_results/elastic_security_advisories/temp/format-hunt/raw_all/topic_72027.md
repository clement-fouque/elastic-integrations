I wanted to bring attention to two blog posts we have done recently in response to the recent set of data ransom attacks affecting Elasticsearch and other systems.  The two are:

* For Elasticsearch: [Protecting Against Attacks that Hold Your Data for Ransom](https://www.elastic.co/blog/protecting-against-attacks-that-hold-your-data-for-ransom)
* For Kibana: [Guarding Kibana from Data Hijacking](https://www.elastic.co/blog/guarding-kibana-from-data-hijacking)

Please read them and react as needed for your install.  The ransom attacks are very real and many in our community have already fallen victim to them.
